# Product Brief: HRAgent

## Executive Summary

**HRAgent** transforms employee administrative work from friction-filled processes into invisible infrastructure. Built for companies with ~200 employees using Factorial HR, HRAgent is a conversational AI assistant that eliminates the cognitive load of timesheets, PTO requests, and policy questions. Rather than forcing employees to adapt to multiple systems, HRAgent adapts to them—learning patterns, preventing conflicts, and turning compliance data into strategic organizational intelligence.

The breakthrough: what starts as an administrative relief system evolves into an organizational operating system. Timesheet data reveals project risks. PTO patterns predict retention issues. Approval workflows surface capacity constraints. HRAgent doesn't just save time (90% reduction in admin burden); it creates strategic insights that were previously invisible. For 200-employee companies, this means $520K+ annual value from time savings alone, plus the transformation of HR from cost center to strategic asset.

---

## Core Vision

### Problem Statement

Employees, managers, and HR staff lose hours each week to administrative friction—not because the work is complex, but because it's scattered across disconnected systems. Sarah (busy employee) context-switches between 6 systems for basic tasks, losing flow and momentum. Marcus (manager) experiences constant low-grade anxiety from untrustworthy systems requiring manual verification. Priya (HR administrator) spends her week firefighting exceptions instead of strategic work.

The real problem isn't that admin takes time—it's that admin creates **cognitive load, vigilance burden, and institutional disrespect**. Current systems communicate "we don't value your time or context." Employees experience this as exhaustion. Managers experience it as bottlenecks. HR experiences it as endless firefighting.

### Problem Impact

**For 200-employee companies, the annual cost is severe:**
- **Employee time loss:** 200 employees × 2 hours/week = 10,400 hours/year ($520K at $50/hr average)
- **Manager bottlenecks:** Approval delays slow decisions and frustrate teams
- **HR inefficiency:** 80% of HR time on tactical firefighting vs. strategic initiatives
- **Hidden costs:** Compliance violations, employee frustration, retention risk from "death by a thousand admin cuts"

The problem compounds: as companies grow, admin burden grows linearly with headcount, but HR teams can't scale proportionally without becoming cost centers.

### Why Existing Solutions Fall Short

**Current approaches miss the mark:**

1. **HR Portals (like Factorial alone):** Consolidate data but still require manual navigation, form-filling, and system-checking rituals. The interface is better, but the friction remains.

2. **Point Solutions:** Each tool solves one problem (timesheets OR policy OR approvals) but creates more fragmentation. Integration burden shifts to employees.

3. **Workflow Automation:** Rules-based systems handle simple cases but fall apart with nuance. They can't adapt, can't explain reasoning, and create brittle processes that frustrate more than help.

4. **Generic AI Assistants:** Lack domain expertise, can't access enterprise systems, provide generic answers without context. They're chatbots, not co-pilots.

**The gap:** No solution combines conversational intelligence + deep system integration + adaptive learning + strategic intelligence generation. That's what HRAgent provides.

### Proposed Solution

**HRAgent is a conversational AI assistant that makes HR administration invisible.**

**Core capabilities:**
- **Replace interfaces with intent:** "Log my hours like last week" replaces 9-field forms with one confirmation
- **Multi-system synthesis:** Combines Factorial HR + calendars + projects + policies into single contextual intelligence
- **Adaptive learning:** Recognizes patterns, personalizes interactions, improves over time per user
- **Preventive architecture:** Detects conflicts, blocks risky requests, explains policy before violations occur
- **Strategic intelligence:** Transforms admin exhaust (timesheets, PTO) into strategic fuel (project risk, retention signals, capacity planning)

**Technical foundation:**
- **Factorial HR integration:** Source of truth for employee data, timesheets, PTO, approvals
- **GPT-5.2-chat:** Conversational reasoning, function calling, risk scoring, intent parsing
- **RAG knowledge base:** Corporate policy interpretation with citation tracking
- **Augmentation architecture:** Intelligence layer separate from Factorial for resilience and evolution

**User experience:**
- Employees: Chat or voice interface from any device, zero portal navigation
- Managers: Pre-approved decisions with veto window, context-rich approval cards
- HR: Clean escalation packages with full audit trails, strategic dashboards vs. firefighting queues

### Key Differentiators

**What makes HRAgent unique:**

1. **Augmentation Architecture:** Unlike tools that replace HR systems, HRAgent augments Factorial HR—respecting the source of truth while adding intelligence that Factorial alone can't provide. This creates resilience, flexibility, and strategic capability beyond basic HRIS functionality.

2. **Admin-to-Intelligence Pipeline:** Every timesheet entry, PTO request, and approval decision feeds predictive models. Compliance data becomes retention signals. Hour logs become project early-warning systems. Administrative exhaust becomes strategic fuel—automatically, without asking users for extra work.

3. **Graceful Uncertainty Handling:** Borrowed from autonomous vehicle design, HRAgent admits when it's uncertain and hands off to humans with full context. "I see conflicting information..." builds more trust than false confidence. This safety-critical approach makes it enterprise-grade, not just a nicer chatbot.

4. **Adaptive Personalization:** Week 1 establishes baseline. Week 4 adapts to Sarah's preferred reminder timing, mobile-first habits, and communication style. System gets MORE helpful and LESS intrusive over time—each user gets their optimal experience without configuration overhead.

5. **First-Mover with Factorial:** As the first AI layer purpose-built for Factorial HR's consolidated platform, HRAgent has structural advantages. While competitors integrate with fragmented systems, HRAgent leverages Factorial's consolidation for cleaner, faster, more reliable intelligence.

**Why now:** GPT-5.2's reasoning capabilities + Factorial's API maturity + post-pandemic remote work friction create the perfect moment. The technology is finally sophisticated enough to deliver on the "invisible infrastructure" promise.

## Target Users

HRAgent serves three equally critical user segments, each experiencing distinct but interconnected pain points:

#### 1. Sarah - The Busy Employee (Marketing Manager)

**Profile:**
- Marketing manager juggling campaigns, meetings, and team collaboration

**Core Pain Points:**

**Key Desires:**
- **Frictionless relief:** One place, one conversation, ideally from phone
- **Pattern intelligence:** System recognizes routines ("log my hours like last week")
- **Gentle guidance:** Error handling that preserves dignity and flow
- **Done-state clarity:** Confidence that tasks are complete and correct
- **Comprehensive context:** All factors considered (balance, calendar, team, projects)

**Success Looks Like:**
- Friday admin drops from 15 minutes across 6 systems to 30 seconds in one conversation
- Proactive nudges prevent missed deadlines without creating anxiety
- PTO requests show immediate impact simulation with auto-mitigations
- System adapts to her patterns, not the other way around

---

#### 2. Marcus - The Manager (Team Lead)

**Profile:**
- Manages team of 8 with competing priorities and tight deadlines
- Approves PTO, timesheets, and exceptions while managing delivery risk
- Experiences constant low-grade anxiety from untrustworthy systems

**Core Pain Points:**
- **Low-grade vigilance:** Constant background anxiety from systems requiring manual verification
- **Systems as liabilities:** Must constantly double-check, not allies
- **False choice:** Trust blindly OR investigate manually—both feel risky
- **Scattered information:** Mental checklist requires data from 5+ systems
- **Fear of critical misses:** One approval mistake could create crisis in two weeks

**Key Desires:**
- **Decision confidence:** Instant context synthesis for every approval
- **Signal not noise:** "Safe to approve" vs "Heads up, this collides with X"
- **Transparent automation:** 80% pre-approval with veto power and reasoning visibility
- **Configurable comfort zones:** Personal thresholds (≤3 days, 70% coverage, etc.)
- **Adaptive learning:** System suggests adjustments based on actual behavior

**Success Looks Like:**
- Approval time drops from 5 hours/week to 30 minutes
- Pre-approved decisions come with 2-hour veto window and full rationale
- Team coverage visualization at a glance—"Team ready for normal operations"
- Stops feeling like bottleneck-administrator, becomes protector-manager

#### 3. Priya - The HR Administrator (Decision Maker)

**Profile:**
- HR administrator responsible for compliance, policy, and employee support
- Gateway between employees, managers, and organizational requirements

**Core Pain Points:**

- **Reconstructing intent:** Forensic investigation after decisions creates risk

**Key Desires:**
- **Policy-first co-pilot:** Empathetic tone, strict logic, not people-pleaser
- **Prevention over remediation:** Stop bad requests early with clear explanations
- **Clean escalations only:** Full story pre-assembled (policy refs, calculations, approvals, reasoning)
- **Audit trail obsession:** Every decision explainable months later
- **Exception management:** Rare, explicit, traceable—not constant firefighting

**Success Looks Like:**
- Exception queue drops from 50/week to 10/week (80% reduction)
- Every escalation arrives with complete compliance package
- Shift from 80% firefighting to 80% strategic work
- HR transforms from cost center to strategic asset with organizational intelligence

**Decision Criteria (as Buyer):**
- Time/cost ROI ($520K annual value for 200 employees)
- Compliance reliability (zero violations, full audit trails)
- Adoption velocity (50%+ usage within 3 months)
- Strategic intelligence value (retention signals, capacity insights)

### User Journey: Sarah's Path to Relief

**Week 1: Discovery & First Win**

*Monday morning:* Priya announces HRAgent during all-hands. Sarah is skeptical ("another tool to learn?") but curious about "one conversation for all admin."

*Wednesday afternoon:* Sarah tries timesheet logging via Slack: "Log my hours - pretty normal week." HRAgent infers from patterns, shows confirmation: "Mon-Fri: 8hrs/day on Marketing Campaign + 2hrs Tuesday on Budget Review. Sound good?" Sarah clicks Yes. **10 seconds total.**

*First reaction:* "Wait, that's it?" Relief mixed with disbelief. Sarah tells three colleagues.

---

**Week 2-3: Trust Building**

*Thursday 3pm:* HRAgent nudges: "Hey Sarah! Friendly reminder to submit your timesheet before Friday EOD. Want me to log it like last week?" Sarah realizes she forgot—sends quick "Yes" from phone. **Crisis averted, zero stress.**

*Friday morning:* Sarah requests PTO for March 10-12. HRAgent responds in 2 seconds: "Checking coverage, calendar, and deadlines... ✅ Looks good! You have 5 meetings that week—I can suggest reschedules. Submit request?" Sarah approves. Marcus gets pre-approval notification with full context, confirms in 30 seconds.

*Trust moment:* Sarah realizes the system actually protects her—it's not just faster, it's safer.

---

**Week 4: Behavioral Adaptation**

HRAgent has learned Sarah's patterns:
- Sends reminders during her 2-3pm low-meeting slot (not 9am when she's deep in work)
- Adapts to mobile-first interactions (shorter messages, one-tap confirmations)
- Recognizes her weekly rhythm (heavy meetings Mon-Wed, focus time Thu-Fri)

*Sarah's reflection:* "It feels like it knows me. Not in a creepy way—in a helpful way."

---

**Month 3: Invisible Infrastructure**

Sarah doesn't think about timesheets, PTO, or policy questions anymore. They just... work. When she mentions admin to new hires, she says: "Oh, just ask HRAgent. It takes care of everything."

**Time savings realized:** 2 hours/week → 10 minutes/week (90% reduction)

**Emotional transformation:** From exhaustion and resentment → peace of mind and trust

**Viral adoption:** Sarah's relief story drives 70% team adoption within first month

---

**Success Signal:** When employees stop talking about admin because it's become invisible—that's when HRAgent has achieved infrastructure status.

## MVP Scope

### Pre-Work: Technical Validation Sprint (Weeks 1-2)

**Critical path activities before MVP development:**

**Factorial API Validation:**
- Obtain Factorial API documentation and test account
- Validate critical capabilities: employee data access, PTO API, webhooks, approval workflows, timesheet entry
- Test performance: rate limits, webhook reliability, response times, data completeness
- Verify data schema: historical data, audit trails, organizational relationships
- **Decision Gate:** GO/NO-GO based on Factorial API capabilities

**RAG Proof of Concept:**
- Test RAG with sample corporate policies
- Measure accuracy on real policy questions
- Validate citation tracking and source attribution
- Confirm acceptable response quality

**GPT-5.2-chat Integration:**
- Prototype basic conversation flow
- Test function calling for Factorial operations
- Validate intent parsing accuracy
- Confirm reasoning capabilities for basic scenarios

**Compliance Review:**
- Legal review of employee data usage (GDPR, labor laws, data residency)
- Define required audit trails and documentation formats
- Establish data retention and privacy policies
- **Output:** Compliance requirements document with constraints

**Stakeholder Interviews:**
- Interview 3-5 employees (Sarah personas) - validate pain points and desired features
- Interview 2-3 managers (Marcus personas) - confirm approval friction and trust requirements
- Interview 1-2 HR staff (Priya personas) - document exception volume and compliance concerns
- **Output:** Validated user needs document, prioritized feature list

**Success Criteria for Pre-Work:**
- ✅ Factorial API delivers core capabilities (employee data, PTO, timesheets, approvals)
- ✅ RAG achieves ≥80% accuracy on policy questions
- ✅ GPT-5.2-chat demonstrates reliable intent parsing
- ✅ Compliance requirements documented with no blockers
- ✅ User interviews validate problem severity and solution approach

**If validation fails:** Evaluate alternative HRIS platforms or adjust MVP scope based on limitations discovered.

### Core MVP Features (Months 1-3)

**The MVP focuses on building trust through reliability before introducing automation.**

#### 1. Conversational PTO Requests

**Capabilities:**
- Submit PTO requests via conversational interface (Slack, Teams, or web chat)
- Check PTO balance and request status
- Natural language date handling ("March 10-12" or "next Friday")
- Immediate feedback on request submission
- Clear confirmation with all details

**Boundaries:**
- ❌ NO auto-approval in MVP
- ❌ NO conflict detection or impact simulation
- ❌ NO calendar integration or meeting rescheduling
- ✅ Basic submission to Factorial → Manager notification → Manual approval

**User Value:** Eliminates portal navigation, reduces friction from 9 form fields to one conversation

---

#### 2. Policy Q&A via RAG

**Capabilities:**
- Answer employee questions about HR policies instantly
- Provide source citations with every answer
- Handle common questions: PTO policies, timesheet requirements, expense rules, benefits
- Clear escalation when uncertain: "I'm not sure about this—let me connect you with HR"
- Track question patterns to improve knowledge base

**Boundaries:**
- ❌ NO personalized policy interpretation (role/location/tenure-specific answers come in Phase 2)
- ❌ NO proactive policy guidance or violation prevention
- ✅ Generic policy answers with citations, human escalation for ambiguity

**User Value:** Instant answers vs. waiting hours/days for HR response, reduces Priya's interruption load

---

#### 3. Manager Approval Notifications

**Capabilities:**
- Context-rich approval cards (not just "approve/deny")
- Show: Employee name, dates requested, PTO balance, reason, policy compliance status
- One-click approval or denial with optional comment
- Clear audit trail of all decisions
- Notification via preferred channel (Slack, Teams, email)

**Boundaries:**
- ❌ NO pre-approval or auto-approval
- ❌ NO risk scoring or confidence signals (Green/Yellow/Red)
- ❌ NO team coverage visualization or delivery impact
- ✅ Manual review with better context than current system

**User Value:** Reduces Marcus's context-gathering time, provides peace of mind with complete information

---

#### 4. Basic Timesheet Logging

**Capabilities:**
- Log hours via conversation: "Log 8 hours today on Marketing Campaign"
- Submit weekly timesheets with simple confirmations
- Check submission status and missing entries
- Reminder notifications before deadline (Wednesday for Friday submission)

**Boundaries:**
- ❌ NO pattern recognition ("log like last week") - comes in Phase 2
- ❌ NO anomaly detection (meeting conflicts, unusual hours)
- ❌ NO project intelligence or burn rate tracking
- ✅ Manual entry made easier through conversation vs. forms

**User Value:** Eliminates portal login and form navigation, mobile-friendly for on-the-go logging

---

#### 5. Foundation Capabilities (Enabling Infrastructure)

**Technical Foundation:**
- Factorial HR integration (read employee data, write PTO/timesheet entries, trigger approvals)
- GPT-5.2-chat conversational layer (intent parsing, function calling, response generation)
- RAG knowledge base (corporate policy indexing, semantic search, citation tracking)
- Authentication & security (SSO integration, role-based access, data encryption)
- Audit logging (complete decision trail, timestamp all actions, immutable records)

**Operational Foundation:**
- Basic analytics dashboard (usage metrics, adoption tracking, error monitoring)
- Error handling and graceful degradation (queue when systems slow, notify on failures)
- Admin interface for Priya (configure policies, monitor exceptions, review audit trails)
